# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that make this project work:

- Being kind and patient with people, especially those newer than you
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for you

Things that are not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at
<vaibhav.mahajan@stepron.com>. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled
privately; the reporter's identity is not shared.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or ban contributors for conduct
they consider inappropriate, and will explain the decision when asked.
